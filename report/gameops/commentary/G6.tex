The guess $\fpguess$ and a challenge $\chall^\circ \sample \cC$ move to
$\mathbf{Initialize}$ (lines $6$ and $7$), and $\chall^\circ$ becomes the
answer to the $\fpguess$-th fresh $\RO$ query (line $12'$).

This is a change of sampling order only: for every fixed value of $\fpguess$
the answers to the fresh $\RO$ queries are independent uniform elements of
$\cC$ in both games, so the joint distribution of $\fpguess$ and of the view of
$\adv$ is unchanged.

This game is exactly the simulation carried out by the impersonator $\bdv$ in
the $\hintdistr$-hint-\textsf{IMP-PA} game: $\bdv_1$ runs $\adv$ until its
$\fpguess$-th fresh $\RO$ query and outputs the commitment appearing in it;
$\bdv_2$ receives $\chall^\circ$, plants it, resumes $\adv$ and returns the
response contained in the forgery. Note that $\bdv$ needs neither a transcript
oracle nor the witness: the $\numOSign$ hints and $\Sim$ suffice.
